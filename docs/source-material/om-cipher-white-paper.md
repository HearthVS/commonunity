## Overview

The OM Cipher is a deterministic identity-sealing algorithm at the heart of the CommonUnity system. It transforms the fixed, immutable facts of a person's birth — date, time, and place — together with their given name, into a multi-layered data structure that encodes who a person is at the level of cosmic architecture. This document explains the algorithm's design principles, its layered computation, and the philosophical and technical methodology by which it can serve as the foundation for an honest-data matching system within the CommonUnity field.

The OM Cipher does not invent a person's identity. It reads coordinates that already exist.

## Part I: Philosophical Foundation

### 1.1 The Fixed and the Living

Every person exists across two registers of information:

**Fixed Information** is the data imprinted at the moment of birth. It does not change. It is not subject to preference, mood, or personal narrative. It can be computed deterministically from three inputs: birth date, birth time, and birth place. This is the domain of the OM Cipher.

**Living Information** is the data accumulated through actual experience — what a person has done, learned, created, loved, suffered, and contributed. It evolves continuously. This is the domain of the CommonUnity Living Profile.

Together, these two registers form what might be called a complete personal dataset — fixed and living, static and dynamic, cosmic and biographical. Like two sides of the same coin, neither is complete without the other. The OM Cipher is the seal on one side. The Living Profile is the record of the other.

### 1.2 The OM as Plasmic Coherence Field

The name "OM Cipher" is not metaphorical decoration. OM in this context is understood as the vibratory field from which form emerges — a coherence field that underlies all structure. The cipher, in the same spirit, is not a disguise or an encryption. It is a *reading* — a structured disclosure of what is already encoded in the moment a person enters physical existence.

This positions the OM Cipher as aligned with the broader CommonUnity philosophy: that honest self-knowledge, rooted in verifiable cosmological data, is the precondition for genuine relationship, collaboration, and unity.

### 1.3 The Template Beneath All Templates

The algorithm is structured around the number 64 — the same organizing principle underlying:

- The 64 hexagrams of the I Ching, the ancient Chinese Book of Changes
- The 64 codons of human DNA (formed by 4 nucleotide bases in groups of 3: 4³ = 64)
- The 64-tetrahedron grid proposed by Nassim Haramein as the fundamental geometry of spacetime
- The 64-bit computational architecture underlying modern digital systems
- The 6-line structure of Gene Keys, producing 64 × 6 = 384 distinct expressions

The OM Cipher treats the 64-hexagram map as the universal template — a Rosetta Stone that connects biological, cosmological, computational, and symbolic dimensions of existence. The algorithm does not impose this structure; it recognizes it as a pre-existing coordinate system.

## Part II: The Algorithm — Layer by Layer

The OM Cipher is computed in six sequential layers. Each layer is deterministic: given the same inputs, the same outputs are always produced. No randomness is introduced at any layer.

**Primary Inputs:**
- Birth Date (DD/MM/YYYY)
- Birth Time (HH:MM, local)
- Birth Place (city, country → latitude/longitude)
- Full Name at Birth

---

### Layer 1: Numerological Root — The Number Body

**Method:** Pythagorean and Chaldean numerology applied to the birth date and full name.

Key computed values:

| Field | Source | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Life Path Number | Birth date digit reduction | Core soul trajectory |
| Expression Number | Full name gematria | Outer character and gifts |
| Soul Urge Number | Vowels of name | Inner motivation |
| Birthday Number | Day of birth digit root | Natural talent |
| Personal Year | Birth date + current year | Current temporal position |

**Rule:** Master numbers 11, 22, and 33 are preserved and not reduced further. These represent amplified or bridging energies in the system.

**Output:** A numeric signature (e.g., Life Path 11, Expression 7, Soul Urge 3) forming the arithmetic skeleton of the cipher.

1. **Personality (Conscious):** The exact position of the Sun and planets at the moment of birth.
2. **Design (Unconscious):** The exact position of the Sun and planets 88 degrees of solar arc prior to birth — approximately 88 days before the birth moment.

Each planetary position is mapped to one of the 64 I Ching hexagrams (each hexagram occupies ~5.625° of the 360° zodiacal wheel) and to one of the 6 lines within that hexagram, producing a gate-line coordinate.

**Key outputs computed:**

| Strategy | How the person is designed to initiate or respond |

Each Gene Key carries three expressions — Shadow, Gift, and Siddhi — representing the spectrum from reactivity to transcendence. The cipher records the gate coordinates; the living profile tracks where on the spectrum the person currently operates.

### Layer 3: Temporal Resonance — I Ching & Cyclical Time

**Method:** The birth moment is mapped into cyclical temporal structures derived from the I Ching and related cosmological systems.

- **Hexagram of Birth Moment:** The I Ching hexagram active at the precise birth time is computed from the lunar calendar position and traditional casting methods.
- **Moving Lines:** Lines in the hexagram that are at a transformative threshold indicate where dynamic change and evolution are concentrated in the person's nature.
- **Octave Position:** Drawing from Gurdjieff's Law of the Octave (Do-Re-Mi-Fa-Sol-La-Si), the birth date is mapped to a position within the octave's seven-tone progression, identifying natural "shock points" where additional energy or support is required for forward movement.
- **Law of Three Position:** The birth is mapped to one of the three forces — Active, Passive, or Reconciling — that the Law of Three describes as the fundamental triad underlying all creation.

**Output:** A temporal-resonance signature indicating the person's natural rhythm, challenge points, and developmental cycle.

### Layer 4: Vibrational Signature — Frequency & Sound

**Method:** A fundamental frequency is derived from the birth data through a deterministic harmonic mapping.

- The Life Path number and the dominant hexagram gate number are combined to derive a root frequency in Hz, mapped to a musical note and its associated Solfeggio family.
- The seed syllable (Bija Mantra) corresponding to the dominant energy center or chakra activation in the Human Design chart is identified.
- The Bhramari / Humming Bee resonance frequency — derived from the fundamental — is computed as the personal toning frequency for somatic calibration.
- Color correspondences are derived from the frequency via Cymatics principles (the visible geometry of sound).

**Output:** A vibrational signature consisting of: root frequency (Hz), musical note, Solfeggio family, seed syllable, Bhramari tone, and color palette.

---

### Layer 5: Name Intelligence — Gematria & Seed Syllable

**Method:** The full birth name is processed through multiple gematria traditions (Hebrew, Greek, English Ordinal) to generate numeric values encoding the name's energetic signature.

- The digital root of the gematria total is computed and cross-referenced with the Life Path number to identify resonance or tension.
- The dominant phoneme of the name is identified and mapped to a seed syllable in the Sanskrit Bija Mantra tradition.
- An **emergent cipher name** is algorithmically generated from a combination of the dominant hexagram, the seed syllable, and the gematria root — a name that may arise in any language or phonetic tradition, unique to this individual. This name is not assigned; it emerges from the computation.

**Output:** Gematria values, name resonance signature, seed syllable, and emergent cipher name.

### Layer 6: Sigil Generation — The Visual Form of the Cipher

**Method:** The sigil is a visual synthesis of all five preceding layers. It is not decorative; it is a geometric translation of the complete cipher data into visual form.

**Inputs to the sigil engine:**

- The Incarnation Cross gate structure (four points on a circle)
- The hexagram line structure (six-line vertical pattern)
- The octave position (angular offset)
- The dominant frequency (determines the geometric family: triangular, square, pentagonal, hexagonal)
- The color palette (derived from vibrational signature)
- The seed syllable (determines the sigil's internal motif or calligraphic seed form)

**Output:** An SVG geometry — a unique, mathematically derived sigil that functions as the person's visual cipher seal. This sigil is:

- Consistent and reproducible (same inputs always produce the same sigil)
- Unique to the individual
- Suitable for use as a personal tattoo, Living Profile avatar, or CommonUnity field handle
- Embedded in the canonical CommonUnity Key (the user's JSON identity file)

## Part III: The CommonUnity Key

All outputs from the six layers are sealed into the **CommonUnity Key** — a lightweight, user-sovereign JSON file named in the format:


This file is the person's portable identity carrier. It is designed to:

The Key is the digital twin of the physical person. The sigil is its symbolic representation.

## Part IV: The Matching Algorithm — Unity in Practice

### 4.1 What "Honest Data" Means

Conventional social and professional matching systems rely on self-reported data — bios, preferences, skills lists — which are inherently subject to aspiration, impression management, and unconscious distortion. The OM Cipher introduces a layer of **honest data**: information that cannot be inflated, fabricated, or strategically presented, because it is computed from immutable coordinates.

Honest data does not replace self-reported data. It grounds it. The fixed cipher provides a baseline against which the living profile can be evaluated for coherence and authenticity.

### 4.2 Complementarity, Not Similarity

The matching algorithm does not seek similarity. It seeks **complementarity** — the principle that functional wholes are composed of distinct but harmonically related parts.

This is modeled directly from the I Ching's hexagram structure: two hexagrams that together form a complete pattern are more generative than two identical hexagrams placed side by side. The Human Design system makes this explicit through the concept of **circuit completion** — that certain gate pairs form channels only when held by two different people in relationship.

**Matching dimensions:**

| Dimension | Basis | What it Seeks |
|---|---|---|
| Gate Complementarity | Human Design channels | Gates that form complete circuits between two people |
| Octave Alignment | Law of Octave position | Complementary positions that support each other's shock points |
| Law of Three Fit | Active / Passive / Reconciling | Triadic role balance in a group or partnership |
| Frequency Harmony | Root frequencies | Consonant intervals (octave, fifth, fourth) between signatures |
| Profile Resonance | Gene Keys line pair | Compatible or mutually supportive profile combinations |
| Living Profile Overlay | Declared skills, intentions, availability | Practical collaboration readiness |

### 4.3 Weighted Scoring

The matching algorithm applies weighted scoring across dimensions, with the following general hierarchy:

1. **Gate Complementarity** (highest weight) — structural energetic fit
2. **Frequency Harmony** — vibrational resonance
3. **Profile Resonance** — behavioral compatibility
4. **Octave / Law of Three fit** — temporal and dynamic alignment
5. **Living Profile overlap** — practical collaboration potential

Weights are not fixed permanently; they are context-sensitive. A match for a creative collaboration will weight frequency harmony and profile resonance more heavily. A match for a functional working team will weight gate complementarity and Law of Three fit more heavily. The user's declared intention at the time of matching query adjusts the weighting model.

### 4.4 The Field Handle

Each member's cipher generates a **Field Handle** — a short, unique identifier in the CommonUnity commons derived from the emergent cipher name and a compressed hexagram code. The Field Handle is the member's public-facing identity token in the field. It is generated by the Sigil builder and carries the frequency and geometric signature of the full cipher in a compressed form.

## Part V: Methodology for Expansion

The OM Cipher is designed as an **open architecture** — a fixed core with defined extension points. The following methodology governs how new information layers can be added without compromising the integrity of the base system:

### 5.1 Principles of Expansion

1. **Fixed data is never overwritten.** Layers 1–6 of the OM Cipher are sealed at onboarding. New layers are always appended, never substituted.
2. **New layers are feature-flagged.** Experimental data layers (e.g., Ayurvedic constitution, biorhythm tracking, astrological transits) are flagged as non-canonical until verified across a sufficient cohort.
3. **The matching algorithm is explicitly versioned.** Every match result carries a version tag identifying which algorithm version and which data layers were active at the time of computation.
4. **User sovereignty is absolute.** No cipher data is shared, projected, or used in matching without the user's explicit, layered consent. The Key's privacy architecture distinguishes between: data visible only to the user, data visible to trusted connections, and data available in the commons field.

### 5.2 Candidate Expansion Layers

| Layer | Data Type | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ayurvedic Constitution (Prakriti) | Self-reported / assessed | Experimental |
| Biorhythm Cycles | Birth date computation | Candidate |
| Astrological Transits | Current planetary positions | Candidate |
| Somatic Measurements | Bhramari tone input, HRV | Experimental |
| Gene Keys Golden Path progression | Self-assessed contemplation depth | Living Profile adjacent |
| Enneagram Type | Self-reported | Supplementary |
| Human Design Variable (Determination, Environment) | Extended chart | Candidate |

### 5.3 Audit Ritual

In alignment with CommonUnity's 8 Limbs governance model, each expansion to the OM Cipher algorithm must pass a milestone audit that asks:

- Does this layer add honest data or self-reported data? (Both are valid, but must be explicitly labelled)
- Does this layer deepen self-knowledge or serve impression management?
- Does including this layer in matching respect the sovereignty of both parties?
- Is the weighting of this layer in the matching algorithm transparent to the user?
