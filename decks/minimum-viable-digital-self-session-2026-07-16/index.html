Where this begins

HEARTH

The oldest technology of gathering — warmth, fire, and belonging at the center of a shared life. The generative context from which CommonUnity emerges.

Why CommonUnity exists

CommonUnity is a space and toolkit for unified living: helping people reclaim their digital lives, deepen self-knowledge, create from inner alignment in trusted community, orient intelligence toward wisdom, and create technology that grows at the speed of trust.

Align the inner. Shape the outer.
